How it works 01 / 04

Sits on top of the window

Runs as a separate overlay process. It draws a filter layer directly above the Roblox window without modifying the game.

Recreates the reshade look

Real-time color grading and contrast. The visual style from BloxShade and RoShade, rebuilt from scratch.

Nothing else

No game modification, no injected scripts. RXShade does exactly one job — apply a visual filter — and nothing else.

Filters 02 / 04

Every filter is an independent HLSL shader pass with its own toggle and 0–100% sliders. They stack in a fixed order, and none of them touch the game — they act on the captured frame on your GPU.

Sharpen
Contrast-adaptive sharpening (CAS-style). Flat areas sharpen more; regions already near clipping are left alone so edges do not ring.
FXAA
Luminance-based edge anti-aliasing. The only AA available when all you have is a finished colour image.
Bloom
Soft-knee bright-pass at quarter resolution, two separable gaussian iterations, additive composite. Light glows instead of clipping flat.
Wet reflection
APPROX — a screen-wide sheen, specular shine on objects, and a floor mirror below a horizon line you position yourself. Stylised, not physically accurate.
Focus
APPROX — radial defocus by distance from screen centre, not by scene depth.
HDR
ACES filmic tone mapping applied in linear light, normalised to its own white point, plus local contrast, highlight recovery and shadow lift.
Colour grading
Exposure, temperature, contrast, saturation and an aspect-corrected vignette, applied in that order.
Chromatic aberration
Transverse lens fringing, weighted by radius so the centre stays sharp. Capped at about 1.2% of frame width.
Film grain
Animated grain quantised into pixel-sized cells, weighted to mid-tones the way real film grain behaves.
Presets one tap
Crisp
Clean and legible. Sharpening plus AA, no colour shift.
Vivid
Punchy, saturated colours with a touch of bloom.
Cinematic
Desaturated and contrasty, with vignette, defocus and grain.
Dreamy
Heavy glow and a shallow sharp centre.
Night
Lifts crushed shadows so dark scenes stay readable.
Water
Wet surfaces — glossy sheen plus a floor mirror.
HDR
ACES tone mapping with strong local contrast.
Why it exists 03 / 04

Tools like BloxShade and RoShade injected files directly into Roblox to hook its renderer. That got them banned.

RXShade takes a different approach. It draws a filter layer on top of the window from the outside, leaving Roblox's process completely untouched.

BloxShade / RoShadeInject files into Roblox to hook the renderer
ResultDetected and banned by Roblox's anti-cheat
RXShadeNo injection — overlays the window from outside the process
